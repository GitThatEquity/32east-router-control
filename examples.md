# Examples
These are illustrative tasks, not additional sources of business facts.

Task: Draft a social-media post.
Answer: 03_Marketing. Router.md lists "Social media" in that route.

Task: Resolve a closing deadline issue.
Answer: 09_TC_Agent. Router.md lists "Closing deadlines" there. The router alone does not establish a particular deal's deadline.

Task: Tell me the company's bank balance.
Answer: Router.md does not provide a balance or a built finance destination. Its numbering scheme reserves 05 for Finance/Accounting. Do not invent a balance or claim that 05_Finance exists.
