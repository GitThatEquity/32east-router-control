## Numbering scheme

The numbers aren't arbitrary — they're banded by where each domain
sits in the business, and the open numbers are reserved for a specific
future domain, not just "next available slot":

| Band | Covers | Folders |
|------|--------|---------|
| 01–03 | **Front office** — revenue-facing, deal-sourcing work | `01_Sales`, `02_Operations`, `03_Marketing` |
| 04–06 | **Systems & support** — internal infrastructure that runs the front office | `04_Automations` (built), `05_` reserved for **Finance/Accounting**, `06_` reserved for **Legal/Compliance** |
| 07–08 | **Deal pipeline** — transaction execution, buy side then sell side | `07_Acquisitions` (built), `08_Dispositions` (built) |
| 09–10 | **Closing & meta** — getting a deal across the line, and tracking the business itself | `09_TC_Agent`, `10_Handoffs` |

When a reserved number gets built out, it should match what's listed
above (e.g. `05_Finance`, `06_Legal`) rather than being repurposed for
something unrelated — that's what keeps the numbering meaningful
instead of just sequential. If a genuinely new domain doesn't fit any
reserved slot, raise it explicitly before assigning it a number rather
than tacking it onto the end.

Each destination folder has its own `Agent.md` with domain-specific
instructions and a list of what's in that folder. For ground rules
that apply across the whole repo, see [`Agent.md`](./Agent.md) at the
root.
